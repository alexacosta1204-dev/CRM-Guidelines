Contacts · Email Segmentation

CRM Contact Tagging Guidelines

Email Campaign Segmentation

To ensure our email campaigns reach the right audience with the right message, every contact in the CRM must be assigned the appropriate TAG(s).

1Important Tagging Rules

  • Every contact should have at least one applicable TAG.
  • A contact can and should have multiple TAGs when more than one applies.
  • Only select tags based on information we actually know about the contact.
  • Tags should be updated whenever we learn new information or the contact's status changes.
  • Do not remove an existing tag simply because another tag is added, unless the old tag is no longer accurate.

2Tag Definitions

TagWhen to Use It
Past ClientContact who has previously closed a mortgage transaction with EMB Lenders.
HomeownerContact who currently owns a home or residential property. This can apply whether or not EMB originated their mortgage.
RealtorContact who is a real estate agent or Realtor.
Event LeadContact obtained through or connected to an EMB event, seminar, presentation, networking event, webinar, etc.
10Yi ClubContact who is currently part of the 10Yi Club/program.
10Yi ProspectContact who has expressed interest in 10Yi or has been identified as a potential 10Yi client but has not yet joined the 10Yi Club.
FTHBFirst-Time Home Buyer. Contact who is considering or actively pursuing the purchase of their first home.
Digital LeadContact generated through a digital marketing source, such as an online funnel, social media campaign, landing page, online ad, or other digital lead-generation campaign.
RenterContact who currently rents their primary residence.
InvestorContact who currently owns investment property OR is interested in purchasing/refinancing real estate for investment purposes.

3Multiple Tags — Use All That Apply

Do not try to select only one category.

Example 1

Maria previously closed with EMB, owns her home, and is now interested in 10Yi.

Tags: Past Client + Homeowner + 10Yi Prospect
Example 2

John came through a Facebook campaign. He currently rents and wants to purchase his first home.

Tags: Digital Lead + Renter + FTHB
Example 3

Carlos attended an EMB event and owns several rental properties.

Tags: Event Lead + Homeowner + Investor
Example 4

Ana closed her home purchase with EMB and later joined the 10Yi Club.

Tags: Past Client + Homeowner + 10Yi Club
Example 5

David came through an online campaign looking for financing to purchase another investment property.

Tags: Digital Lead + Investor

4Tags That Can Change Over Time

Some tags represent a stage and should be updated as the relationship progresses.

10Yi

10Yi Prospect → 10Yi Club

Once the contact officially becomes part of the 10Yi Club, remove 10Yi Prospect and add 10Yi Club.

First-Time Home Buyer

A contact may initially be:

Digital Lead + Renter + FTHB

After purchasing and closing with EMB:

Past Client + Homeowner

At that point, Renter and FTHB should be removed because they no longer describe the contact's current situation.

5Before Saving or Updating a Contact

Ask yourself:

Has this person closed with EMB before?
Past Client
Do they currently own a home?
Homeowner
Do they currently rent?
Renter
Are they a first-time buyer?
FTHB
Are they a Realtor?
Realtor
Are they an investor or interested in investment properties?
Investor
Did we obtain them through an event?
Event Lead
Did they come from digital marketing?
Digital Lead
Are they a potential 10Yi client?
10Yi Prospect
Are they already part of the 10Yi Club?
10Yi Club
Golden Rule

Tag what you know — and use all tags that apply.

Accurate tagging allows EMB Lenders to properly segment our database, personalize our communication, and make sure each contact receives information that is relevant to their needs and relationship with us.

CRM Contact Sourcing Guidelines

Lead Attribution & Source Tracking

To ensure every contact and opportunity in the CRM is properly attributed, the SOURCE field must identify where or through whom the lead originally came to EMB Lenders.

Accurate sourcing is important for:

  • Marketing and lead-generation reporting
  • Measuring the performance of our referral channels
  • Identifying our strongest business sources
  • Tracking team member production and lead generation
  • Determining applicable lead-generation attribution and compensation
Golden Rule

SOURCE = Who or what originally generated the lead for EMB Lenders.

The person currently working the lead or originating the mortgage is not automatically the Source.

1Where Source Lives in GoHighLevel

Source is recorded in two places on the opportunity card. Both should be filled in:

Opportunity details → Source (free-text field)
Type what kind of source it is, using the exact values in the table below (e.g. BDO, EAM, BDO-Form).
Team Information tab (dropdowns)
Select who: Equity Asset Manager, Business Developer (BDO), Loan Officer, Realtor, Referral (Other than Realtor), Builder/Developer.

Example: Source BDO tells us the lead came from a BDO; the Business Developer (BDO) dropdown in Team Information tells us which one.

2Source Definitions

SourceWhen to Use It
LOLead was personally generated by an EMB Loan Originator.
EAMLead was personally generated by an EMB Equity Asset Manager.
BDOLead was personally generated by an EMB Business Development Officer.
BDO-FormLead was booked through the 10Yi Booking Hub form. This is filled in automatically when the form is submitted — no need to type it.
EAM-FormAn EAM booked their own lead through the 10Yi Booking Hub. The form still saves the Source as BDO-Form, so the EAM must open the card and change it to EAM-Form.
RealtorLead was referred to EMB by a Realtor or Real Estate Agent.
Business PartnerLead was referred by an external professional, company, or strategic business partner.
Past ClientLead or referral was generated by a previous EMB client.
AccountLead came through an established EMB business/strategic account, such as an employer, hospital, real estate office, association, etc.
EventLead was generated through an EMB event, seminar, webinar, presentation, expo, or similar activity.
Digital MarketingLead was generated through digital advertising, social media campaigns, funnels, landing pages, or other paid/online marketing efforts.
Website / OrganicLead contacted EMB directly through the website, organic online search, or other non-paid web activity.
NetworkLead originated through personal or professional networking and no more specific source applies.
OtherUse only when none of the established Source options apply. Additional details should always be provided.

3Recording the Specific Person, Account, or Campaign

There is no separate "Source Detail" field. Whenever possible, record the specific person, organization, campaign, or event in the matching field on the card:

Source: Business Partner
Team Information → Referral (Other than Realtor): John Smith – ABC Insurance
Source: Event
Opportunity details → Event Information: EMB Homebuyer Seminar – August 2026
Source: Account
Comments/Notes: Memorial Hospital – Employee Program
Source: Digital Marketing
Comments/Notes: Meta – First-Time Homebuyer Campaign

4EMB Team Member Lead Attribution

When the lead is generated by an EMB team member, it is especially important to identify who generated the lead. Type the role in Source, select your name in the matching Team Information dropdown, and set yourself as the card's Owner.

Example — a BDO logs their own lead:

Source: BDO
Owner: [BDO Name]
Followers: [EAM they booked with]
Team Information → Business Developer (BDO): [BDO Name]
Team Information → Equity Asset Manager: [EAM they booked with]

or

Source: EAM
Team Information → Equity Asset Manager: [EAM Name]

or

Source: LO
Team Information → Loan Officer: [LO Name]

Setting yourself as Owner lets you filter the pipeline by owner to track your own clients. Adding the EAM as a Follower lets them see that the client is theirs too, so they can follow up and keep it moving.

This information may be used for internal lead attribution, reporting, and applicable compensation.

5Source Is Not the Same as Assigned LO

Do not change the Source simply because a different team member is handling the mortgage.

Example: A BDO develops a relationship with a hospital. The hospital refers an employee to EMB, and the mortgage is assigned to an LO.

The CRM should reflect:

Source: Account
Comments/Notes: Hospital / Employer Name
Team Information → Business Developer (BDO): [BDO Name]
Team Information → Loan Officer: [LO Name]

The LO handling the transaction did not become the original Source of the lead.

Changing it to EAM-Form is the only extra step. It tells the team the asset manager, not a BDO, is the Source.

An LO personally brings a borrower to EMB.

A Realtor sends their buyer to EMB.

Source: Realtor
Team Information → Realtor: [Realtor Name / Company]
Example 5 — Digital Lead

A consumer completes an EMB Facebook/Instagram funnel.

Source: Digital Marketing

Applicable contact TAGS may separately include:

Digital Lead + Renter + FTHB

If an EMB BDO or other team member developed the account, also select them in the matching Team Information dropdown.

7Important Sourcing Rules

  • Every new lead/contact should have a SOURCE whenever the information is known.
  • Select the Source based on who or what originally generated the lead.
  • Do not change Source simply because the lead is reassigned to another LO, EAM, processor, or team member.
  • Record the specific person, company, event, account, or campaign in the matching Team Information field, Event Information, or Comments/Notes whenever it can be identified. Ongoing client updates go in the Notes tab instead.
  • For internally generated leads, always select the EMB team member responsible in the Team Information tab.
  • If an EAM books through the 10Yi Booking Hub, change the Source from BDO-Form to EAM-Form.
  • Do not guess the Source. If uncertain, verify before selecting it.
  • Use Other only when none of the established Source categories apply.
  • Because Source information may affect lead attribution and compensation, changes should be made carefully and only when correcting inaccurate information.

8Source vs. Tag

Remember that SOURCE and TAGS serve different purposes.

SOURCE tells us
Where did this lead come from?
TAGS tell us
Who is this contact and what do we know about them?

Example: A person responds to a Meta advertisement. They currently rent and want to purchase their first home.

Source: Digital Marketing
Comments/Notes: Meta – FTHB Campaign

Tags:

Digital Lead + Renter + FTHB

9Before Saving the Contact, Ask

1. Who or what originally brought this lead to EMB?
Type the appropriate SOURCE
2. Can I identify the specific person, account, campaign, event, or organization?
Record it in Team Information, Event Information, or Comments/Notes
3. Did an EMB team member generate the lead or relationship?
Select them in TEAM INFORMATION and set them as OWNER
4. Did this come through the 10Yi Booking Hub?
Leave BDO-Form, or change it to EAM-Form if an EAM booked it
5. Who will actually work/originate the mortgage?
Select the Loan Officer in Team Information, without changing the original Source
Final Rule

Always preserve the original source of the lead.

Accurate sourcing allows EMB Lenders to know where our business is coming from, who is generating it, which relationships are producing, and who should receive proper attribution.

Opportunities · GoHighLevel

EAM/LO Checklist

Required Fields for Opportunity Cards

Before saving or moving an opportunity, every Equity Asset Manager must confirm the fields below are complete and accurate on the GoHighLevel opportunity card. A blank field is not the same as "not applicable" — if it applies and it's unfilled, the card isn't done.

Primary Contact Required

  • Enter the client's first and last name, email, and phone number.
  • The Primary Contact must be the person the loan is for — the borrower — not simply whoever happens to be the point of contact.
  • If someone else is coordinating on the borrower's behalf (spouse, family member, assistant, etc.), add them under Additional Contacts, not as the Primary Contact.

Opportunity Details Required

  • Opportunity name is the client's full name
  • Owner is the team member who brought in the lead (e.g. the BDO) — lets them filter the pipeline by owner to track their clients
  • Followers include the EAM the client is booked with, so they can see the client is theirs too
  • Opportunity Type is set to New Business or Existing Business

Source Required

The SOURCE field must be filled out on every opportunity — e.g. BDO or EAM when entered manually, BDO-Form when booked through the 10Yi Booking Hub. If an EAM books through the Booking Hub, change it to EAM-Form.

📎 See the full Contact Sourcing Guidelines →

Team Information Required where applicable

Confirm the following dropdowns are filled out in the Team Information tab:

  • Equity Asset Manager — the EAM the client is booked with
  • Business Developer (BDO) — if applicable; BDOs select their own name
  • Equity Asset Analyst
  • Loan Officer
  • Realtor
  • Referral (Other than Realtor) — if there is one
  • Builder/Developer — if applicable

After the outcome meeting, update this tab with whoever the client is moving forward with — the Loan Officer, Analyst, and Realtor.

Loan Information Required

  • Loan Inquiry Form is attached / completed
  • LOAN ID# is entered — once the client has an application in Arive, copy the Loan ID shown on their Arive file and paste it here
  • Any other details you see in Arive that match a field here (Loan Type, Loan Purpose, Type of Property, Occupancy, etc.) are filled in

The Loan ID is used to automate updates and keep track of clients across Arive and GoHighLevel.

10Yi Fields Required

  • 10Yi field is marked Yes or No — never left blank. Any client booked for a 20-minute intro meeting is Yes
  • Booking Date is the date of the 20-minute intro meeting you booked
  • Outcome Date is the date of the outcome meeting, filled in the same way once it is booked
  • 10Yi Strategy is filled in

For any 10Yi prospect, the entire 10Yi Program Information tab must be filled out.

Notes Required

Every update about the client belongs in the Notes tab of the opportunity card — not only in an email, text, or chat. If you would write it to a teammate, log it as a note too, so anyone who opens the card can see where things stand.

Add a note whenever you:

  • Talk to or follow up with the client — what was discussed and what happens next
  • Learn something about their qualification — credit score, income, debt-to-income, credit issues to fix
  • Decide on or change the loan plan — e.g. restructure, refinance, HELOC, or second mortgage
  • Bring in or update someone else — Realtor, LO, Analyst — and what they know or agreed to
  • Are waiting on something — documents, a signed package, a callback — and the date it's expected
  • Get an update on outstanding conditions or documents — why they're delayed and when to expect them

Emailing the LO, EAM, or processor is fine, but the same update must also be logged as a note. An email only reaches the people on it; a note is visible to everyone working the file.

Example note — qualification
Spoke with client today. FICO 640, income $4,200/mo, DTI 52% — needs to pay down two cards before we can proceed. Realtor (Ana) is aware. Plan: revisit for a cash-out refi once DTI is under 45%. Client will send updated statements by Fri 11/6.
Example note — documents delayed
Followed up on pending conditions (bank statements + W-2s). Client is out of town until Sunday; committed to uploading everything by Wed 11/4. I'll check in Thu 11/5 if nothing has come in. LO notified.

Keep notes short, factual, and dated by what's next. The Comments/Notes field on Opportunity details is only for a one-line detail (like a campaign or account name); ongoing updates always go in the Notes tab.

Booking the 20-Minute Intro Meeting

There are two ways to book:

  • 10Yi Booking Hub (recommended): open the link from the email titled "Your 10Yi Booking Hub" (check your inbox, Other, and Junk) — add it to your phone's home screen to book on the go. Choose the asset manager, enter the client's first and last name, phone, and email, select your name, choose New or Existing Business, and add any useful notes (homeowner, financial details, etc.). Press Submit, then pick a time slot from the EAM's availability. The opportunity is created in GoHighLevel automatically with Source BDO-Form.
  • Manually in GoHighLevel: open the opportunity, click the contact icon to go to the contact page, then in the right-side menu click Appointments → Add appointment. Choose the calendar of the EAM you're booking with and pick an available time slot — the contact's name is added automatically.

Either way, you still need to fill in the Booking Date on the card.

Moving Through the Pipeline

StageWho moves it & when
01. 1st Contact / Interview (24 hrs)New opportunity — fill in contact, opportunity details, Source, Team Information, and 10Yi fields.
02. 20 Min Intro MeetingMove here once the meeting is booked and the Booking Date is filled in. This tells the EAM your part is done; BDOs don't need to enter any more data after this step.
03. Gathering Docs (1–3 days)The EAM moves the card here after the intro meeting if the client wants to move forward, and requests documents in Arive.
04. 10Yi – Under Analysis (24–48 hrs)Moved here once documents are received. From here the Equity Asset Analyst moves the card forward when the analysis is done.
05. 10Yi – Outcome (24–48 hrs)The EAM books the outcome meeting and fills in the Outcome Date.
06. 10Yi – ImplementationThe EAM moves the card here if the client wants to move forward after the outcome. From then on, the Analyst moves the card forward.

The times in each stage name show how fast a card should be moving. If a card has sat in a stage longer than that, reach out to the EAM — or, if they're having trouble getting documents, reach out to the client yourself to help keep it moving.

Golden Rule

An opportunity card isn't complete until every field above reflects what we actually know.

Incomplete Primary Contact, Opportunity Details, Source, Team, Loan, 10Yi, or Notes make it impossible to report accurately or attribute production correctly — make it a habit to go through every tab before moving a card to the next stage.
